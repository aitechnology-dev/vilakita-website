About Vila Kita

Something for everyone, under one roof.

Vila Kita is designed for family holidays, reunions and groups of friends. The recreational facilities are all inside the house, so guests do not need to look for activities elsewhere.

The covered swimming pool can be enjoyed during the day without worrying about the heat. The pool table room, movie room and karaoke are perfect for the afternoon and evening. All bedrooms are air-conditioned.

Around Temerloh

Nearby attractions.

Driving distance and time from Vila Kita.

Kuala Gandah Elephant Conservation Centre

An elephant conservation centre run by PERHILITAN (the Department of Wildlife). Great for a visit with children. Check the opening hours before you go.

34.2 km · 40 min

Temerloh Esplanade Food Stalls

A row of food stalls along the Pahang River. Temerloh is famous for ikan patin tempoyak, silver catfish cooked in fermented durian.

12.2 km · 15 min

Temerloh Sunday Market

Known locally as Pekan Sehari. A weekly Sunday market selling farm produce, river fish, food and traditional kuih.

12.3 km · 15 min

Go' Bang Maju Patin Tempoyak

One of the best-known patin tempoyak restaurants in Temerloh, in Kampung Raja. Serves fresh patin fish and Malay village-style dishes.

9.2 km · 13 min

Kancil Raja Patin

A patin restaurant on the Temerloh–Jerantut road, known for its spicier pais patin (patin grilled in banana leaf). Come early, as dishes often sell out before the afternoon.

7.2 km · 10 min

Selera Patin Bangau

A village eatery in Kampung Bangau, near the Pahang River. A good choice for patin tempoyak in a kampung setting.

14.6 km · 20 min

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Where is Vila Kita located?

Casa Hill, 28400 Mentakab, Pahang. About 5 minutes from Mentakab town and 15 minutes from Temerloh town. Use the Waze or Google Maps buttons in the Location section.

What facilities are provided?

A covered swimming pool, movie and karaoke room, air-conditioned pool table room, bubble dome on the outdoor deck, bedrooms with queen beds, a family room with a children's play tent, and a kitchen with an island table.

Is the swimming pool covered?

Yes. The pool can be used when it rains and is shaded from the midday sun.

Is it suitable for families with young children?

Yes. There is a family room with a play tent and plenty of indoor activities. Children must be supervised at the swimming pool.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3:00 pm and check-out is by 12:00 noon.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A security deposit of RM200 is required and is refunded after check-out if there is no damage or loss.

How do I make a booking?

Choose your dates on the booking calendar at the top of the page. Only available dates can be selected. Then send your dates and number of guests via WhatsApp, and we will confirm your booking and let you know the rate.

Is Vila Kita open?

Yes. Vila Kita is now open and accepting bookings. Check available dates on the booking calendar or contact us via WhatsApp.
